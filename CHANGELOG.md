# Changelog

## 0.4.0 · 2026-10-08

- Five reference signal families, a bounded declarative strategy program, causal exit policies, loss-budget sizing and editable research recipes with rejection criteria.
- Shared-capital historical portfolios across 2–10 aligned spot/perpetual markets, sequential next-open rebalancing, explicit residuals/rejections and independent chronological test windows.
- One-use pre-registered holdouts, alias/warmup access guards and project trial records.
- Incremental forward bars and indicator checkpoints, hash-bound decisions, actual account equity observations and a durable controllable synthetic clock.
- Content-addressed compressed artifacts and shared candidate inputs; bounded queue, plan admission and selected-detail projection.
- Disposable computation processes with progress, deadlines, maintenance cancellation, owner-death cleanup and Linux CPU/additional-address-space limits. No workspace credentials enter the child environment.
- Persistent operational incidents, acknowledgement/recovery, supervisor progress, backup failure/age and low-disk conditions.
- Desktop/mobile workflows, bilingual controls, deferred research page bundles and verified schema-4 recovery of the new evidence chain.

See [verification](verification.md), [strategy workflows](strategy-workflows.md) and the [current red-team review](audit/README.md). Financial model assumptions and substantive remaining product gaps are explicit; the release does not claim investment edge, exchange matching fidelity or industry-leading maturity.
