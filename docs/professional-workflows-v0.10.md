# From a reviewed hypothesis to an accountable paper book

Tidebench v0.10 closes the three reproduced operational defects in the [fresh v0.9 review](audit/fresh-institutional-review-0.9.0.zh-CN.md): unavailable funding no longer blocks protection; historical and forward groups share the same failure contract; retained inventory cannot disappear behind healthy process status. Further review found and corrected fee-adjusted capital admission, contract-unit funding allocation, negative-margin admission, omission of known poor L2 samples, manual-capital declaration admission and zero-budget maintenance rebalances. These are correctness contracts, not a declaration of investment edge.

## Research a falsifiable hypothesis

Use the source-backed strategy dossiers and published negative results, then save an immutable definition. Study its costs, neighboring parameters and independent periods. The static basket supports fixed weights, independent signals, momentum, risk-budgeted momentum and matched funding carry. Portfolio execution now freezes `reduce_group_v1`, the residual threshold and failure policy; raw older studies retain their recorded legacy contract rather than acquiring a new result under an old hash.

The result explains actual monetary price P&L, fees, funding and recorded fill-to-quote shortfall. Its accounting identity must reconcile. Per-market contributions are unavailable when typed lifecycle accounting requires additional event-aware reconstruction. A predeclared passive underlying-price reference is adjusted for entry fee, lot, tick and minimum size; perpetual trade prices are explicitly proxies. Exposure differences and descriptive full-window beta are shown, never called risk-matched alpha. Missing evidence yields unavailable values, not zero.

For changing membership, select **Historical lifecycle** and import per-leg attributed source JSON. The engine accepts distinct verified listing windows, causal warmup, finite metadata coverage, suspension/resume, delisting, actual USDT cash settlement and exact same-identity linear-contract unit conversion. Inspect eligibility, source hashes, native inventory journals and unresolved events. [Formats and supported accounting](historical-lifecycle.md). Supplied facts cover the selected markets and intervals; an announcement is not complete venue membership history. Historical lifecycle studies are blocked from current-rule managed release because that controller does not execute historical settlements or conversions. This boundary is enforced, not an acknowledgement checkbox.

## Review the whole account before adding risk

Each running portfolio first reserves a durable percentage of current equity. Concurrent requests cannot both promise the same capital. Effective admission sums, per owner, the greater of actual use plus pending orders and the promised percentage. The base-asset policy sums absolute spot and perpetual exposure; a BTC long/short pair cannot conceal BTC gross concentration through delta netting.

Every new-risk order rechecks admission in its financial transaction against projected post-fill equity:

`equity_after = equity_before − fee + signed_base_quantity × (mark − fill_price)`

Spot capital is its marked inventory value; derivative capital is posted initial margin. Fill-to-mark loss and fees lower the denominator immediately. Cash planning reserves the entire entry cost, not only a fee scaled by the portfolio percentage. Account order-size, gross, same-base concentration and daily-loss policies still apply. Changing a bound capital policy invalidates earlier approved deployment conditions.

Small maintenance adjustments below venue size or below the remaining cash allowance can retain existing same-side inventory within the separately reviewed residual limit. They cannot manufacture a missing new leg or permit a reversal. Over-limit residuals still fail the group and enter compensation. Historical and forward adapters use the same ordered commands and deterministic splitting, with actual-account parity counterexamples.

**Stop** stops the controller. It does not mean the inventory has been sold. The capital promise remains while inventory, pending commands or unresolved funding survive. Inspect group attention, retained quantities and current valuation, then issue protective reductions. Capital is released only on verified flatness.

## Resolve economic incidents

The trading desk separates process health from economic health. Acknowledgement records operator attention; it cannot remove current exposure, unresolved funding or damaged ownership. A failed flat group remains a warning until an explicit Stop disposition. Compensating inventory and unverifiable ownership are critical. Missing or stale marks display unknown monetary inventory values, with actual quantities preserved.

Funding is attached to settlement-time quantity, contract metadata and owner shares. Publication gaps retain obligations while permitting reduce-only exits and simulated mark liquidation. A later published rate settles the old inventory exactly once, including after close/reopen or restart. Closed exposure charges cash; retained original-generation base exposure charges isolated margin. Contract-unit changes use economic base units, not a ratio of old and new contract counts. Unresolved funding prevents a complete equity/return claim and new risk, rather than inventing a zero fee.

## Examine measured costs and forward evidence

**Operations → Cost and depth** captures public decoded OKX L2 data, receipt clocks, metadata, raw-data hashes and deterministic finite-depth VWAP. Native units, lot/minimum rounding, partial depth exhaustion and both sides are explicit. Calibration checks independent exchange timestamps, real elapsed time, cadence, freshness, participation and worst-sample shortfall. Existing captures inside the declared window cannot be omitted to hide a known bad sample. Frozen public reports are pinned by identity/hash into the portfolio review. They inform a local-paper review; they are not queue models, venue fills, historical impact or future capacity guarantees. Trading fees remain separate.

**Execution → Forward performance** computes statistics over the entire selected observation window; chart pagination cannot hide early drawdowns or gaps. Freeze fixes observation IDs, ordered hashes, audit boundary, wall time and acceptance rules; verification recomputes that same window. Missing valuations, funding publication gaps, uncovered time, external flows without contemporaneous valuations, restore events and ledger/cost counter regressions prevent a spurious continuous strategy return. [Evidence contract and thresholds](forward-evidence.md).

The real-time acceptance requires 14 actual days, bound public-market clocks, sufficient observations, economic coverage and observed recovery evidence. Advancing synthetic time or a short smoke run cannot satisfy elapsed days. Even a passing account-observation report establishes neither HTTP availability nor investment edge.

## Recover without rewriting the evidence

Schema 8 backups validate capital, funding, lifecycle, public depth and frozen-report identities, including pinned capture references. Recovery preserves later non-financial research facts, instrument observations, lifecycle source events and public depth captures/calibrations. It replaces the financial epoch, halts accounts, stops controllers, cancels pending commands/orders and revokes sessions. Later financial observations, lifecycle accounting applications and performance reports remain with the later financial epoch in the safety backup; they are not grafted onto the older balance sheet.

No exchange credentials are accepted. All execution remains local paper. One SQLite writer/process lease is an explicit deployment boundary; tenant isolation, distributed execution and exchange routing are outside this product's implemented scope.
